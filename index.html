Site P&L — with central cost allocated by area, ongoing sites only

Fee income = agreement value × the project's commission rate (per-project, editable in the Projects tab below). Central marketing & CP-sourcing costs are spread only across sites marked Active (see Status below — that's how the system knows a site is "ongoing"), proportional to each site's Total area (sqft). A site marked Upcoming or Completed shows ₹0 allocated — it isn't sharing the current central cost. An Active site with no area set gets ₹0 share of its own (0 ÷ total area) unless none of the Active sites have an area set yet, in which case the cost splits equally among them as a placeholder — so fill in Total area for every live site to get a true area-proportional split.

All bookings — cancel, raise commission bill, mark received

Status dropdown offers Booked / Agreement Done / Cancelled — change it to "Agreement Done" to unlock "Raise bill" on that row. Bill amount = agreement value × the project's commission rate (per-project, editable in the Projects tab). "Mark received" appears once a bill has been raised. Commission due / Commission bill are both about Realtech's own commission, not whether the customer has paid the developer — Due means the bill hasn't been received yet (whether or not it's been raised), Received means it has.
